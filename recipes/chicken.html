Chicken Wings

Description

Tasty wings with a hint of honey and the smokey delicious flavor of Tabasco Chipotle.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 lbs chicken wings (about 8)
  • 4-5 cups canola oil
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons TABASCO® brand Chipotle Pepper Sauce

Directions

  1. Combine flour, salt and pepper in a medium-sized plastic bag.
  2. Remove tips from chicken wings and discard; cut remaining wings in half.
  3. Place chicken pieces one at a time in the bag and shake to coat well
  4. Remove wings to a clean piece of parchment or waxed paper and set aside
  5. Heat 2 inches of oil in a deep-sided skillet to 350 degrees; fry chicken in batches until crisp and golden brown, turning occasionally, 12-15 minutes.
  6. Once fried, place chicken on parchment-lined baking sheet and keep warm in the oven.
  7. Melt butter. Add the honey and TABASCO® Chipotle Sauce to hot butter and stir until well combined.
  8. Transfer to a large bowl.
  9. Add the wings and toss to coat.
  10. Serve immediately.
